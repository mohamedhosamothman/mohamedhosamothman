## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst & BI Developer** with 3+ years of experience across **facilities management, debt collection, and sales**, and a background in **Electronics & Communication Engineering**.

🏗️ I currently work as a **Data Analyst on Land Sterling's consulting team**, supporting **Tatweer Buildings Company (TBC)** — building and maintaining an enterprise-grade facilities platform covering **~3,700 government schools** across the Western Region of Saudi Arabia.

🤖 Beyond dashboards, I design and ship **full-stack web systems** — from an embedded **RAG-powered AI assistant** grounded in official technical guides, to internal tools with **role-based access control**, **custom JWT authentication**, and **live Supabase/Google Sheets-backed pipelines**.

💡 My work blends **technical precision with storytelling** — turning raw, messy data into clean datasets, live dashboards, and systems that management actually uses to make decisions.

Enterprise-grade facilities platform covering **~3,700 government schools** with 20+ live modules (condition scores, complaints, maintenance, security, spare parts, fleet, and more), each backed by a Google Apps Script API layer with caching and retry logic. Includes **"AI Core,"** a RAG-powered conversational assistant grounded in Saudi Arabia's National Facilities & Asset Management Guide, letting management query live data and official standards securely.
`HTML` `JavaScript` `Chart.js` `Google Apps Script` `OpenAI GPT API` `RAG`

### 🗂️ Admin Operations System — *Full-Stack Web App*
Role-based internal admin system (vehicles, fuel, petty cash, expenses, IT assets) with a static front end, Supabase backend, **RLS-enforced permissions**, **custom RPC/JWT authentication**, and exportable CSV reports per module.
`HTML/JavaScript` `Supabase` `RLS` `JWT`

### 🌴 Leave Management System — *Full-Stack Web App*
Complete leave management web app built from scratch for internal HR use, with a Supabase backend, Excel import/export (SheetJS, ExcelJS), and a bilingual **RTL Arabic interface**.
`HTML/JavaScript` `Supabase`
